Settings

These details print on every document. Set them once.

Business profile

Home state decides CGST+SGST (same state) vs IGST (other state) on each document. CodeMetrix = Odisha (21).

Payment details

Your UPI and bank (NEFT) details print on every bill and quotation so clients can pay. The QR image is stored in this browser and travels with your backup.

Payment QR image

Upload the BHIM / UPI QR screenshot from your bank app (a square PNG works best). It appears on the printed documents under "Scan & pay".

Bank transfer / NEFT

Service centres

Declare each centre as an additional place of business on your GST registration. The code becomes the invoice-number prefix (e.g. CM/BAL/…).

Data

All data is stored only in this browser. Back it up regularly; restore moves it to another machine.
